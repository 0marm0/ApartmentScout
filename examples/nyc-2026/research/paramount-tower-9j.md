# Paramount Tower #9J: the October 8 benchmark

Checked October 8, 2026. [Listing](https://streeteasy.com/building/paramount-tower/9j),
[owner listing 6983](https://www.glenwoodnyc.com/listing-detail/?lid=6983),
[J-line plan, floors 3-52](https://glenwoodadmin.com/webdav/images/floorplans/paramount/PM3-52J.jpg).

- $4,895 gross after an October 5 price cut, advertised as available now.
- J line: bedroom 18'0" x 11'5" (an unusually large bedroom), living room 20'2" x 12'5", kitchen 7'9" x 7'1".
  The three rooms add up to about 511 sq ft. A manual tracing of the inside of the exterior walls gave about
  660-680 sq ft of interior floor (broad range 650-700). No washer/dryer symbol on the plan.
- The same J-line drawing was used by an earlier fit check for a different unit; the A-line (41A) and
  K-line (40K, 49K) drawings for floors 3-52 are modelled too, so the lines can be compared side by side.

## What the comparison found

The question was whether any one-bedroom with an elevator, a staffed lobby and a gym beat Paramount on size
without giving up building quality, views, location or cost. Short answer: yes on space, less certain on
everything together.

| Unit | Rent | Advertised size | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corinthian #27F | $4,950 | 774 sq ft | 27th floor, East River views, bedroom terrace; condo fees unknown |
| Corinthian #21J | $5,200 | 903 sq ft | 21st floor, east, balcony; at the ceiling before fees |
| Kips Bay Towers #9K | $4,600 | 900 sq ft | north views, large dining area; 1960s building; application $225 + $50 |
| View 34 #S19J | $4,997 | 740 sq ft | required charges $163 a month bring it to about $5,160 |
| Murray Hill Marquis #2001 | $5,025 | 709 sq ft | marginal size difference |
| Horizon #15K | $4,500 | 700 sq ft | equal size, renovated |

Advertised gross areas and traced interior areas are different measurements, so no percentage enlargement
was claimed. Several building-level leads (Horizon #30C, Highpoint #20D) had plans without dimensions or
attachments that belonged to another building, so their size stayed unresolved.

Plans of the other K and A lines show the trade-offs inside one building: K (floors 3-52) has a 21'10" living
room and 16'4" bedroom; A (floors 3-52) is a corner line with a 19'4" x 15'6" living room that has windows on
two sides and a kitchen window.
