## Rent regulation and historical legal context

A [2017 NYC financing document](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/hyic/downloads/hyic-2017ab-os.pdf) lists Emerald Green with a 20-year 421-a tax exemption. HCR's older building lists also include the address. These are meaningful building-level signals; they do not establish #1211's exact present lease status or the exemption's end date.

Request a written statement of #1211's stabilization status, legal regulated rent, any preferential rent, complete vacancy rider, and 421-a benefit/surcharge schedule. [HCR explains](https://hcr.ny.gov/surcharges-and-fees) that certain older 421-a programs permit separately disclosed surcharges during benefit phase-out, using a specific historical rent basis. **Do not simply add 2.2% of $5,021.** Ask whether the advertised number already includes any surcharge and whether a charge changes during the initial lease. [HCR records access](https://hcr.ny.gov/node/9991) generally belongs to the subject tenant/owner or an authorized representative; request an owner-provided certified apartment history before signing rather than assuming prospective-tenant access.

Openigloo's generalized Good Cause badge was not adopted as a unit-specific legal conclusion. The actual exemption/status and lease documents govern.

The [2016 DOJ consent decree](https://www.justice.gov/d9/glenwoodsettle_2_11_16.pdf) lists Emerald Green among additional properties in Glenwood's accessibility case. [DOJ's case summary](https://www.justice.gov/crt/case/us-v-glenwood-management-corp-sd-ny) describes audits and proposed retrofits beyond Liberty Plaza. This is historical accessibility context, not evidence of an unresolved violation in #1211 today. No claim is made that the accessible public feeds capture every private lawsuit.

## The exact questions to settle before applying

1. Is #1211 still available for October 16, and what is the ordinary-month total? Resolve $5,021 versus $5,022 and gross versus net wording.
2. Does its bedroom fully close with a permanent door? Send current continuous video, dimensioned plan, usable interior area and compass exposure.
3. Which supplied photos/video depict #1211? Show open-blind daylight views, appliances and any wear or planned refurbishment.
4. What are all mandatory charges and the gym/pool/lounge membership price? Is fitness alone free? Provide hours, guest rules and closure plans.
5. What is its rent-regulation status, legal rent, preferential rent if any, 421-a surcharge and benefit expiry? Is every collectible charge included in the quote?
6. Provide the current bedbug disclosure and latest annual filing. Any recent incidents in #1211, adjacent units or the same floor?
7. What garage/facade repairs remain? Any scaffolding, noise, roof closure or planned work facing #1211? Provide dates rather than verbal assurances.
8. How does its bedroom face refuse pickup, loading docks, restaurants, mechanical plant or construction? When are collections?
9. Confirm heating/electric/AC billing, internet providers and typical recent utility bills for this unit/line. What maintenance or equipment replacement is scheduled?
10. Will the employment offer letter meet income verification? Is remote touring/application allowed, and is a guarantor needed? Confirm deposit, insurance, application and move-in terms.

Please send an itemized written quote including all mandatory charges, any concession and any 421-a surcharge, plus the gym/pool membership options. **Decision rule:** tour promptly if they can document privacy and cost. Proceed only if the actual bedroom is quiet enough for sleep, the daylight/view suits you, and total rent plus selected amenities fits your budget. Reconsider if they cannot establish the bedroom enclosure, itemize collectible charges, or disclose scheduled disruptive work.
