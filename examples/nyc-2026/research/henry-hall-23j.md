Recommendation: **keep 23J on the shortlist, but make the decision conditional on an actual-unit inspection and written answers to the documented concerns below.** There is enough here to warrant serious diligence; there is not evidence establishing that 23J is defective, infested or unsafe.

## Lease costs and protections: settle the exact unit

At $5,100 for 643 advertised sq ft, the arithmetic is about **$95.18 per sq ft annually** and **$61,200 annual base rent**. These are calculations, not a market appraisal. A recurring $100 fee adds $1,200 annually. At the advertised deposit of one month's rent, first month plus deposit is about $10,200 before application fees and other applicable costs; actual signing/proration terms must be quoted.

Building-level evidence supports asking seriously about stabilization. Latest DOF exemption data checked in the records appendix shows lots 1304/1305 with active code 5116, identified as a 20-year 421-a(15) benefit, starting 2020. This **does not determine 23J's current regulated status, legal rent or precise expiration**. Get its rider or documented exemption, legal versus preferential rent, any concession treatment and applicable surcharge. Do not accept a generic promise that increases will be reasonable. [HCR tax-benefit guidance](https://hcr.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2022/07/fact-sheet-41-07-2022.pdf), [HCR surcharge guidance](https://hcr.ny.gov/surcharges-and-fees).

Obtain a complete quote listing every required recurring, usage-based, optional and move-in/out charge: amenities, package/trash/utility billing, internet, insurance, pets, bike/storage, reservation and moving fees. The application/deposit/electricity disclosures do not prove that every situational charge is zero. Ask whether October 15 possession is accepted and whether rent starts earlier. Current availability alone does not answer that.

## A focused inspection of 23J

1. **Inspect the exact apartment.** Confirm the number, current video/photo provenance and condition; measure usable bed/desk/sofa clearances, column and hallway. The combined living/kitchen plan dimensions are not a separate clear living-room measurement.
2. **Test systems.** Run HVAC at different settings, check vent airflow and bathroom exhaust, operate faucets/shower simultaneously, and test washer/dryer and dishwasher. Ask specifically about 24J's ventilation complaint and winter heat/hot-water repairs. Record observed noise/odors without assuming their source.
3. **Listen and smell.** Spend quiet time in each room with windows closed, HVAC off then on; listen for hum, alarms, traffic/horns, neighbors and door slams. Inspect floor-23 corridor and refuse room for smoke and cleanliness.
4. **Check service operation.** Ride elevators at a busy time, note waits, inspect posted certificates, and ask how many passenger cars serve floor 23. Observe guest verification, package logging and after-hours repair handling.
5. **Inspect shared facilities.** Test gym equipment and see rooftop/lounge condition, access rules, charges and planned closures. Confirm how commercial-tenant visitors and deliveries are separated from residents.
6. **Document defects.** Obtain a signed move-in condition record with photographs of floors, counters, bathroom fixtures, mirrors, appliances and windows. Get promised repairs and completion dates in writing.
7. **Walk your daily routes.** Test weekday/evening transit, groceries and waterfront access. Your comfort with the street and its traffic is more useful than a broad neighborhood safety label.

- Provide surrounding-work notices, past storm/water-entry history and the building's backup-power/elevator outage plan.

Discuss directly with at least two current residents if possible, preferably on higher floors/J-line, about the last winter, summer HVAC, repair turnaround, smoke, elevator waits and the present team. Such conversations were not conducted for this research.

**Decision rule:** Proceed if 23J passes a weekday systems/noise/odor inspection, current residents support the service claims, and management supplies coherent written answers and lease terms. Pause if it refuses the current disclosures/compliance information, minimizes the nearby ventilation complaint without a repair explanation, or the exact unit has persistent smoke, poor airflow, mechanical noise or water damage.
