# Emerald Green #620 (line 20)

Checked October 14, 2026. Line 20 on the sixth floor of Emerald Green (320 West 38th Street), compared for
furniture fit in an earlier study. Price and availability were not captured.

- Glenwood's line-20 plan prints a living room of 18'11" x 12'0" and a bedroom of 13'7" x 10'0", with a
  washer/dryer closet, a linen closet, a walk-in closet off the foyer and a galley kitchen with a pantry.
  Traced at the printed sizes the apartment is about 590-640 sq ft.
- The plan's north arrow puts the window wall on the south-southwest side, the same facade as the #1211
  study. The 3D site model therefore uses 209 degrees with the plan as its source (not an assumption).
- At floor 6 the window is low against the 37th Street block: about 2 h of direct sun a day on average and
  almost none in December and January. Floor 15 in the same line would get several hours more on October 15.
- The plan file has two presets that fit cleanly: bed on the outside wall with a 55-inch TV on the partition,
  or bed on the partition just past the door swing.
