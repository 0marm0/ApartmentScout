# Henry Hall: reviews, ownership and lease questions

Checked September 30, 2026. Public allegations are not adjudicated facts. No residents or management were contacted.

## Current ownership changes the interpretation of older reviews

[JLL's March 3, 2026 transaction announcement](https://www.jll.com/en-us/newsroom/jll-capital-markets-arranges-129m-sale-and-71m-financing-of-henry-hall) identifies Amstar Group LLC as buyer for $129 million, Shorenstein Investment Advisers/Dreamscape Companies as sellers, and $71 million in acquisition financing through U.S. Bank. This is a transaction participant's primary announcement, not an audited assessment of operating quality. The loan does not establish financial distress.

[Current StreetEasy building information](https://streeteasy.com/building/henry-hall) identifies Canvas Property Group as management/marketing/leasing team. Current listings call Canvas the landlord's exclusive broker/agent. Review platforms and older HPD registration records still identify Greystar. Obtain the actual current operating manager, legal landlord, management start date and deposit-accounting contact in writing. A sale does not prove either improvement or deterioration.

## Google Maps: directly inspected in the browser

[Henry Hall Apartments public Google Maps profile](https://www.google.com/maps/place/?q=place_id:ChIJ7QDM00xYwokRcAS3k4AlkfU) at 515 W 38th was opened through Google Maps search. The profile displayed **4.3/5 from 111 reviews**: 82 five-star, 10 four-star, 2 three-star, 2 two-star and 15 one-star. Thus 17/111, about 15.3%, were one- or two-star ratings; this is the share of posted reviews, not the share of unhappy residents. Google explicitly labels reviews unverified. The newest view and lowest-rating view were inspected, including the 15 one-star and 2 two-star entries loaded in that order; selected longer reviews were expanded. This was not a census of all 111 full texts.

The newest three entries were ratings without narratives: 5 stars two weeks ago, 5 stars one month ago, 4 stars one month ago. More substantive favorable reviews praise staff, maintenance, windows, layouts and cleanliness, but often predate the sale. A favorable staff experience and an unfavorable accounting/repair experience can both be real.

## Other review platforms: concise evidence checks

[ApartmentRatings](https://www.apartmentratings.com/ny/new-york/henry-hall-apartments_9199332346275178882/) shows 4.2/5, 36 reviews, 26 marked verified. Its verification includes surveyed prospects, not only established residents. September 2025 feedback praises service and window sound insulation; March 2025 reports shared-space upkeep/disorganization; August 2024 criticizes gym equipment. An August 2024 negative review alleges smoke and poor management. Older positive feedback is plentiful. Do not count cross-posted resident reviews as independent corroboration.

[Openigloo](https://www.openigloo.com/building/nyc/1090727-1007107501/manhattan-chelsea-515-west-38-street-10018) shows 4.1/5 from only five reviews and 60% recommendation/owner approval. Reports mention train horns, neighbor/dog noise, smoke, HVAC and visitor/package security. Its displayed relative review timestamp is unstable, so no precise publication date is inferred. Its headline says one open violation while the class breakdown is all zero; it says no litigation/bedbug history despite positive official historical records. These summaries cannot replace primary records.

[Birdeye](https://reviews.birdeye.com/henry-hall-apartments-156775759157076) shows 4.2/154 aggregated reviews, inconsistent with the directly inspected current Google count; treat it as an older/different snapshot. [RentCafe](https://www.rentcafe.com/apartments/ny/new-york/henry-hall/default.aspx) and [Point2Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Apartment-Buildings/NY/New-York/Henry-Hall.html) display a management-company score based on approximately 17,800 reviews across properties. ## Commercial tenants and amenities

[Indienne's own site](https://www.indiennenyc.com/about) confirms its restaurant on Henry Hall's second floor. Old references to Legacy Records room service should be reconfirmed. Restaurant convenience is a benefit; separate resident access, visitor routing, trash/loading, odors, events and any resident discounts require current confirmation. Restaurant activity does not establish noise in a 23rd-floor unit.

The [building amenities page](https://henryhallnyc.com/amenities/) advertises a jam room, fitness center, rooftop and social spaces. Verify operational condition and access terms. A historical listing for [15F](https://streeteasy.com/property/9854960-henry-hall-15f) advertises stabilization and no amenity fee; that is useful context but not a current 23J promise.

## Regulatory and economic questions

[2019 Manhattan Community Board 4 material](https://www.nyc.gov/html/mancb4/downloads/pdf/november-2019/29-updates-to-mcb4-affordable-housing-plan-no-memo.pdf) identifies 225 apartments including 46 affordable units and permanent affordability for that component. [FY2018/19 DOF 421-a list](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/finance/downloads/pdf/421a/1819/421a_1819_manhattan.pdf) lists 509 W38 condominium lots 1304 (179 residences) and 1305 (46 residences). These are historical building-level evidence, not 23J's current regulatory determination or benefit-expiration date.

Ask for 23J's current rent-stabilization rider or written basis for exemption, legal regulated rent versus actual preferential/base rent, concession treatment at renewal, any 421-a expiration notice/surcharge and its calculation, and one-/two-year lease terms. HCR explains [tax-benefit regulation](https://hcr.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2022/07/fact-sheet-41-07-2022.pdf) and [surcharges](https://hcr.ny.gov/surcharges-and-fees); their applicability here remains unverified. Owners can furnish [certified apartment rent histories](https://hcr.ny.gov/node/9991); a prospective renter cannot assume unrestricted access to another tenant's records.

## Historical owner litigation

Official published decisions identify construction-worker injury litigation involving HY 38 Owner LLC: [Lima, September 14, 2022](https://www.nycourts.gov/courts/AD2/Handdowns/2022/Decisions/D69712.pdf), involving an alleged April 2015 caisson-pipe injury, and [Rodriguez, March 10, 2021](https://www.nycourts.gov/reporter/3dseries/2021/2021_01436.htm), involving a construction-site gate injury. These concern construction work and procedural liability issues. They do not establish current unsafe residential conditions, a tenant judgment or a defect in 23J. Name searches are not a complete NYSCEF/eCourts docket audit, and entity association alone cannot identify every suit's property.

## What the evidence supports

Retain 23J as a serious candidate, with conditions. Review evidence is mixed rather than uniformly bad. The meaningful recurring checks are responsive maintenance/accounting, smoke control, security procedures, and actual acoustic performance. Verify the present team and exact unit instead of relying on the lobby, average rating, a generic no-fee advertisement or an older tenant's unit.
