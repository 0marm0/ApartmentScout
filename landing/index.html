Features

Everything a careful renter checks,
for every listing at once.

It came out of a real Manhattan search. The questions were the same for every finalist, so the answers became tools.

Map & commute heat

See every minute of the trip, not a radius.

A built-in transit router turns any city's GTFS feed into door-to-door minutes on a 150 m grid and for every listing, walking legs included. The map shows where your limit really ends.

  • Commute bands under the rivers, labelled in minutes
  • Route bullets and legs for each listing
  • 311 noise, rent per ft² and score surfaces
Explore the map →

Rank on your criteria

Your weights. Live re-ranking.

Budget tiers are judged on true monthly cost: net-effective rent plus every fee. Drag a weight and every card and pin re-ranks while you drag. Unknown facts show as uncertainty, never as a guess.

  • Must-haves, preferences and commute limits in one file
  • Each weight shown as its share of the score
  • Why a listing ranks where it does, dimension by dimension
Open the shortlist →

Building records

Read the building before the lease.

One command pulls the city's records for your shortlist: HPD violations and complaints, DOB and ECB filings, permits on the block, bedbug reports, 311 noise and nearby crime. The agent adds reviews from Google, Yelp, Reddit and more.

  • Sourced dossiers, dated and linked
  • Reports are labelled as reports, not findings
  • Hour-of-day noise profiles per building
Read a dossier →

Light & view in 3D

Will the window see the sky or a wall?

Real footprints and heights around the unit, live sun and shadows for any date and time, and the view from the window itself. Compare floors and exposures in direct-sun hours by month.

  • Sun path against the skyline from the window
  • Direct-sun hours for every floor and month
  • Exposure worked out from listing photos and video
Open Unit 3D →

Floor plans

Walk the plan, with the sun coming in.

A short JSON description of a listing plan becomes a 3D apartment. Sun patches fall through the windows at the right angle for the move-in date, blocked by the buildings across the street.

  • From a plan image, PDF or stated room sizes
  • Any date and time of day
  • Bird's-eye and top-down views
Open the floor plan →

Compare & tours

Finalists side by side, then a decision.

Put finalists next to each other on cost, size, commute, light, records and reviews. Plan tours with night and noise checks, capture what you learn and finish with a decision memo.

  • Best value in each row highlighted
  • Tour checklists and open questions per unit
  • Saved picks export as a file you keep

Ask in plain language. The playbooks do the rest.

Open the repo in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any agent that reads AGENTS.md. It interviews you for what is missing, writes your criteria and works through nine stage-by-stage skills. Plain Markdown, so any agent can follow them.

How it works

Plain files in, a dashboard out.

Every stage is a CLI command over files in your search folder, so you, your agent or a spreadsheet can step in anywhere.

  1. 01Criteriacriteria.yaml

    Budget tiers, must-haves, commute limits and weights.

  2. 02Listingsimport

    CSV, JSON or your agent, each fact with a source.

  3. 03Geocodegeocode

    Coordinates, plus BBL and BIN in New York.

  4. 04Commutecommute

    Door-to-door minutes from the GTFS timetable.

  5. 05Recordsdossier

    City records and reviews for the shortlist.

  6. 06Sitesite

    Footprints, heights, sun hours and the view.

  7. 07Scorescore

    A ranked shortlist on your weights, unknowns shown.

  8. 08Dashboardbuild

    Static files you serve, host or open as one HTML file.

Quick start

Running in a minute.

Clone, install and open the worked example. Then start your own search in any city with a GTFS feed.

  • Python 3.10 or newer
  • No API keys, accounts or servers
  • Building-records automation for New York; a short module adds a city
Try the worked example
git clone __REPO__.git
cd ApartmentScout
pip install -e .
apartmentscout serve --search examples/nyc-2026
Start your own search
apartmentscout init searches/my-search --city nyc
export APARTMENTSCOUT_SEARCH=searches/my-search
apartmentscout import my-listings.csv
apartmentscout all        # geocode, commute, layers, score, build
apartmentscout dossier    # building records for the shortlist
apartmentscout serve
Or one self-contained file
apartmentscout build --standalone my-search.html

Privacy & responsible use

Your search stays yours.

  • Local by default

    Searches live in a git-ignored folder. Criteria, notes and saved picks stay on your machine unless you publish them.

  • Respect for sites

    Agents use official APIs and open data, never solve CAPTCHAs or bypass bot checks, and ask before logging in anywhere.

  • Reports, not findings

    311 calls, complaints and reviews are allegations. They are counted, dated and labelled that way everywhere.

  • Estimates, checked in person

    Commute, sun and score numbers are models. Nothing is sent to a landlord without your say-so; verify the important ones on a visit.
