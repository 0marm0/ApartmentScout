# Tour checklist: <Building> <Unit>   (<date>, <time>)

Bring: compass app, tape measure, phone speaker, charger, the listing and this list.

## Confirm on site
- [ ] Unit number, floor and which way the main windows face (compass app at the window)
- [ ] Measure the bedroom and living room; does the bed / sofa / desk fit?
- [ ] Bedroom has a door and a window
- [ ] Washer/dryer in unit and working; dryer vented or condensing
- [ ] Water pressure and hot water time (kitchen and shower)
- [ ] HVAC on: how loud in the bedroom? off: street noise with windows closed, then open
- [ ] Phone signal in each room
- [ ] Closets and storage
- [ ] Light at this time of day vs the model's prediction
- [ ] Hallway, elevator wait, package room, trash room condition
- [ ] Amenities actually open and their hours/fees

## Ask
- [ ] Open questions from the research note: <paste>
- [ ] Concessions, fees, start date in writing
